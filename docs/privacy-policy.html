Tap Tap Cricket - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Tap Tap Cricket is an offline cricket game. We built it so that it does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings (sound, batting hand, colour theme, hints) and your best scores in a small file on your own device so they are there next time. This file never leaves your device. Deleting the game deletes it.

Permissions

  • Vibration (Android): short vibrations for wickets and boundaries. You can turn this off in Settings.

The game does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or any other personal data.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version added online features), the new policy will be published here before that version is released.
